# Question review — October 2026

Every question, answer, round instruction and tiebreaker in the app was checked three ways:

1. **Read-through** of all 1,462 questions and 48 tiebreakers against known facts.
2. **Automated checks**: link-a-letter rounds still spell their mystery phrase, linked rounds (initial letters, chain letters, sums, connections) still work, multiple-choice options never repeat the answer, no duplicate new questions.
3. **Web verification** of anything time-sensitive or doubtful. Where a fact could not be confirmed, the question was rewritten around a fact that could.

## What changed

- **Wrong answers fixed** (examples): planet with most moons is now Saturn; Myrrh is a tree resin, not from orchids; Indira Gandhi first became PM in 1966; Carrie Symonds was born before Lara Johnson; Wales now has seven cities; Davis Cup question replaced (France had only three wins); Ronnie Biggs escaped in 1965; Rubik's Cube record in Jan 2017 was 4.73s.
- **Out-of-date questions** rewritten with dates ("In December 2020…") instead of "recently" or "due to", so they stay true.
- **Misleading or unprovable claims** removed (for example, "the only human in The Muppet Christmas Carol").
- **Spelling and names** corrected (Nicolas Cage, Kenny Dalglish, Savile Row, Alan Shepard, Esmeralda and others).
- **Round instructions** cleaned. Internal editor notes such as "Preserve answer order…" no longer show on the TV.
- **Questions that relied on the round heading** (population pairs, who was born first, lakes, film taglines) now carry the full question, so each one makes sense on its own.
- **Built-in quizzes renamed**: The Friday Classic, The Sunday Social, The Big Night In, The Local Legend.
- **Two new original quizzes** added: *The Crown & Anchor* and *The Rose & Crown* (5 rounds of 10 multiple-choice questions each, plus 2 tiebreakers each).

Every individual edit is listed in `QUESTION_CHANGES_OCT2026.csv` (ID, field, before, after).
